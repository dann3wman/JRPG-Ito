Stage three used both preceding stages. The third stage received one framing correction; the retained PNG is the corrected output. Files were copied without raster processing. Existing assets and prototype code were preserved.

All three retained outputs are 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNGs, rather than the requested 1536 square. Each has alpha values from 0 to 255. Browser-rendered comparison on paper and charcoal backgrounds was inspected for silhouette, connected anatomy, clipping, palette continuity and transparency.
