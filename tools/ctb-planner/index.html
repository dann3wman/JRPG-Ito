CTB Planner

Humming Crystal · turn-order design tool

Lay out a fight's tempo before scripting it. Each row acts in a cycle of Delay values; the planner predicts the turn order with the same rules as the battle prototype (tick speed max(8, 30 − Speed), 12 ticks to a slot, party first on ties, Lost Turns from Danger 30). Use it for the bosses that are tempo puzzles: Forty Years, the Whisperer, the Dazzling Country, the Listener's Eye. The URL holds the whole plan, so paste it into a boss spec.
